01 About

I started in the classroom as a social studies teacher and campus technology coordinator. That experience shaped how I think about learning, adoption, and human behavior.

After 10+ years in SaaS and EdTech—from Compass Learning to Tableau to Juniper Square—I'm now focused on building tools that leverage AI to help people think better and create more effectively.

Currently working on DailyScribe, a voice-to-publish pipeline, and Art of Fact, teaching non-technical professionals how to actually use AI through systems thinking.
